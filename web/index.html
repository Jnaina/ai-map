About Jag's AI Map

An interactive map of the AI companies, models, products, people, hardware and ideas that are getting the most attention in English-language AI news and developer communities. Bigger, closer to the centre and more connected means more talked about.

How heat works

Every story adds heat to each entity it mentions, weighted by source and popularity (Hacker News points, Hugging Face upvotes, GitHub stars). Heat cools with a 24-hour half-life, so the map reflects the last few days of a 7-day window. Links join entities that appear in the same stories. New (orange ring) marks entities first spotted this week; ↑ means heat is at least 1.5× what it was 24 hours earlier, ↓ that it has cooled. Topics are drawn as diamonds; the hottest topic is filled orange and its closest connections are outlined.
